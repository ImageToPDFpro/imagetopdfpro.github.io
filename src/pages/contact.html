Before you write

  • Where are my files? We never receive your images or PDFs. Everything is processed on your own device, so we can't recover a file for you. Simply convert it again.
  • The converter isn't working. Make sure you are using an up-to-date version of Chrome, Safari, Edge or Firefox, then refresh the page.
  • My PDF is too large. Choose "Balanced" or "Small file" under Image quality. See how to reduce PDF file size.

For privacy-related requests, please read our Privacy Policy first. It explains what data is and isn't collected.
