Why convert images to PDF?

Photos are great for sharing moments, but they are awkward for documents. Send someone eight separate pictures of a signed form and they have to open, rotate and scroll through each one. Put the same pictures into a PDF and they receive one file, in the right order, that looks identical on every phone, tablet and computer and prints at the correct size.

That is why banks, universities, employers and government portals usually ask for PDF uploads. A PDF keeps pages together, preserves orientation and supports standard paper sizes such as A4 and US Letter. It is also easy to archive, search for and attach to email.

Common reasons people use this converter

  • Applications and forms: scanned ID cards, certificates, mark sheets and signed documents for online submissions.
  • Receipts and expenses: a month of receipt photos combined into one expense report.
  • Study notes: photos of whiteboards, handwritten notes or textbook pages turned into a single study pack.
  • Work documents: screenshots, diagrams and design mockups shared as one reviewable file.
  • Memories: photo collections, recipes or kids' drawings saved as a printable booklet.

Tips for the best-looking PDF

  1. Shoot in good light. For documents, daylight near a window beats a flash, which creates glare.
  2. Keep the camera parallel to the page to avoid a tilted, trapezoid look.
  3. Pick the right fit. "Fit whole image" never crops anything. "Fill page" removes white borders but may trim the edges.
  4. Use "Same as image" page size for screenshots and artwork where you don't want any white space.
  5. Compress only when needed. Keep "Original" for printing and archiving. Switch to "Balanced" or "Small file" when a website has an upload limit.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is this image to PDF converter really free?

Yes. There are no limits on files or pages, no watermark, no trial and no sign-up. The site is supported by advertising, which lets us keep the converter free for everyone.

Are my images uploaded to a server?

No. The conversion happens entirely inside your web browser using your device's own processing power. Your images are never sent over the internet, so we can't see, store or share them. You can even turn off your internet connection after the page loads and it will still work.

Will converting to PDF reduce the image quality?

Not with the default "Original" setting. JPEG images are copied into the PDF exactly as they are, and PNG, WebP and other formats are stored losslessly. Quality only changes if you pick "High", "Balanced" or "Small file" to make the PDF smaller.

How do I convert iPhone HEIC photos to PDF?

Just add them like any other picture. HEIC and HEIF files are detected and converted automatically, even in browsers such as Chrome and Firefox that can't display HEIC on their own. Conversion of HEIC photos takes a few extra seconds. See our iPhone photo guide for more tips.

How many images can I put in one PDF?

There is no fixed limit. Because everything runs on your device, the practical limit depends on its memory. Computers handle hundreds of photos easily. On older phones, very large batches may need the "Balanced" quality setting.

How do I change the order of the pages?

Drag an image card to a new position. On phones and tablets, drag using the handle in the top-right corner of each card. You can also use the arrow buttons under each image, or sort everything by file name or date from the Sort menu.

Can I make the PDF smaller for email or an upload portal?

Yes. Set Image quality to "Balanced" for email or "Small file" for websites with strict limits such as 1 MB or 2 MB. Photos taken on modern phones usually shrink by 70–90% while staying perfectly readable.

Does it work on Android and iPhone?

Yes. It works in Chrome, Safari, Edge, Firefox and Samsung Internet on phones, tablets and computers. On mobile you can pick photos straight from your gallery or take a new photo with the camera.

Why is my photo sideways in the preview?

Some cameras save orientation information that not every app reads the same way. Tap the rotate button on the card until the preview looks right. The PDF will match the preview exactly.

Can I install it as an app?

Yes. In Chrome or Edge, use "Install app" from the address bar or menu. On iPhone, tap Share and then "Add to Home Screen". The installed app opens instantly and works offline.
