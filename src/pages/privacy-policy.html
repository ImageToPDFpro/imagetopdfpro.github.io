1. Your images and PDF files

The Service works entirely inside your web browser. When you add images and create a PDF:

  • Your files are read and processed locally on your device.
  • Your images and the resulting PDF are never uploaded to our servers or to any third party.
  • We have no access to the content, names or metadata of your files.
  • Files are held only in your browser's temporary memory and are discarded when you start over, close the tab or leave the page.

If you add iPhone HEIC photos, your browser may download an open-source decoding library from this Website. The decoder runs on your device; your photos are not sent anywhere.

If you install the Website as an app on Android and use the system "Share" menu to send images to it, the shared images are passed to the Website's offline storage on your own device for a moment so that the converter can open them, and are deleted immediately afterwards.
